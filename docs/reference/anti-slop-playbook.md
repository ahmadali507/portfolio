Master these five pillars:1. The 3D Asset & Baking Pipeline (The #1 Anti-Slop Secret)Real-time lights and shadows in WebGL look flat, plastic, and computationally expensive. High-end 3D portfolios (like Bruno Simon's or Award-winning studio sites) rarely rely on raw dynamic Three.js lights.Blender UV Unwrapping & Lightmap Baking: Learn to bake global illumination, ambient occlusion, and complex caustics directly into high-efficiency texture maps in Blender using Cycles, then apply them to MeshBasicMaterial in Three.js for $120\text{ FPS}$ photorealism on mobile devices.Compression Pipeline (gltf-transform): Master CLI asset optimization:Draco / Meshopt for geometry compression ($70\%\text{–}90\%$ smaller files).KTX2 / Basis Universal (toktx) for GPU-compressed textures that stay compressed in VRAM, preventing mobile browser crashes.2. Custom Shaders (GLSL & TSL)Standard PBR materials (MeshStandardMaterial) look the same on every website. Custom shaders give your portfolio a signature visual identity.Vertex Shaders: Deform meshes dynamically based on scroll velocity, cursor proximity, or simplex/curl noise (e.g., cloth ripples on project image planes, liquid terrain).Fragment Shaders: Build custom procedural textures, halftone/dithering effects, iridescent thin-film interference, or SDF (Signed Distance Field) shapes.Post-Processing Passes: Use pmndrs/postprocessing to write custom screen-space effects—subtle film grain, lens distortion at viewport edges, custom LUT color grading, or ASCII/pixelation transitions.3. Hybrid DOM + WebGL Synchronization ("The Curtain Effect")A hallmark of amateur 3D portfolios is placing a <canvas> behind standard HTML cards where the two layers feel completely disconnected.DOM-to-WebGL Plane Mapping: Track the bounding rectangles (getBoundingClientRect()) of standard HTML <img> and <section> tags, and render matching 3D planes inside the WebGL canvas at the exact same pixel coordinates. This allows your project thumbnails to bend in 3D space on scroll while keeping standard HTML accessibility and SEO intact (popularized by libraries like curtains.js or custom R3F viewport portals).Unified Smooth Scrolling: Pair Lenis (studio-freight/lenis) with GSAP ScrollTrigger and Three.js's requestAnimationFrame loop so scroll inertia drives camera spline progress (CatmullRomCurve3) and shader uniforms (uScrollVelocity) with zero jitter.4. Spatial Storytelling & Diegetic UIInstead of building a standard webpage that happens to have a 3D model in the corner, design a cohesive concept:Diegetic Interfaces: Put your portfolio projects inside the 3D world—for example, rendered onto the CRT monitor of a retro 3D computer (using RenderTarget or <Html transform> in Drei), printed as physical cartridges the user picks up, or projected onto architectural walls.Physics & Tactile Play: Integrate lightweight WebAssembly physics (Rapier via @react-three/rapier) so visitors can knock over typography, drag interactive lanyards (like the Vercel ship badge), or drive/walk through an environment.Audio Design (Howler.js / Web Audio API): Subtle spatial audio—low ambient room tone, mechanical clicks on hover, whooshes tied to camera velocity—elevates a 3D site from a tech demo to an experience (always provide a prominent mute toggle).5. Performance Engineering & Graceful DegradationNothing ruins a 3D portfolio faster than a laptop fan spinning to maximum volume at $14\text{ FPS}$.Adaptive DPR (Device Pixel Ratio): Use <AdaptiveDpr /> or detect-gpu (pmndrs/detect-gpu) to dynamically lower resolution or disable heavy post-processing passes when frame times exceed $16.6\text{ms}$ ($60\text{ FPS}$).On-Demand Rendering: If the scene is static until the user scrolls or moves their mouse, set frameloop="demand" in React Three Fiber so the GPU rests at $0\%$ utilization when idle.Instancing & BatchedMesh: Group repeated geometry (foliage, particles, architectural details, grid tiles) into a single THREE.InstancedMesh or THREE.BatchedMesh to keep draw calls under $100$.Part 4: The Anti-Slop Workflow (DESIGN.md Template)The most effective workflow used by top creative developers who leverage AI tools is the Contract-First Workflow: Never prompt an agent to build a page before locking in a DESIGN.md and ARCHITECTURE.md file in your repo root.Copy and customize the template below into your project root as DESIGN.md so any AI assistant you use is strictly bound to your aesthetic and 3D performance rules:# DESIGN.md — Anti-Slop & 3D Architecture Contract

## 1. Aesthetic Direction & Anti-Patterns (STRICT)
- **Visual Persona:** [e.g., Industrial Swiss Brutalism / Tactile Retro-Futurism / Warm Editorial Gallery]
- **BANNED UI PATTERNS (Immediate Reject):**
  - NO `Inter`, `Roboto`, or system-ui default fonts.
  - NO purple/indigo/blue-to-pink gradients (`from-purple-500`, `to-indigo-600`, etc.).
  - NO generic 3-column symmetrical feature cards with `rounded-xl` and `shadow-lg`.
  - NO glowing neon borders or unprompted glassmorphism (`backdrop-blur`) on cards.
  - NO decorative floating 3D primitives (no aimlessly spinning torus knots or icosahedrons).
  - NO pill badges with sparkle emojis (`✦`) above section headings.

## 2. Typography & Color Tokens
- **Display Font:** [e.g., 'Syne' or 'PP Neue Montreal'], tracking `-0.03em`, line-height `0.95`.
- **Body Font:** [e.g., 'Plus Jakarta Sans'], tracking `0em`, line-height `1.5`.
- **Technical / HUD Monospace:** [e.g., 'JetBrains Mono'], uppercase, tracking `+0.08em`, size `11px`.
- **Palette:**
  - Canvas / Background: `#0E0E0C` (Warm Obsidian)
  - Primary Surface: `#161613`
  - Primary Text: `#F2EFE9` (Bone White)
  - Muted Text: `#8C8881`
  - Accent (Used on < 5% of viewport): `#FF3E00` (International Orange)

## 3. 3D & WebGL Engineering Rules
- **Stack:** Three.js (r170+) / React Three Fiber / Drei / GSAP / Lenis.
- **Asset Budget:**
  - Total scene triangle count MUST stay under 150,000 tris.
  - All `.glb` models MUST use Draco/Meshopt compression and KTX2 textures.
  - Draw calls MUST remain under 80 per frame (use `InstancedMesh` for duplicates).
- **Lighting & Shaders:**
  - Prefer baked textures (`MeshBasicMaterial`) or custom GLSL/TSL `ShaderMaterial` over multiple dynamic shadow-casting lights.
  - Every animation or camera movement must be tied to user intent (scroll progress, pointer raycasting, or state transition) with damped interpolation (`THREE.MathUtils.damp`).
- **Memory Management:**
  - Explicitly dispose of geometries, materials, and render targets on unmount.
  - Never instantiate `new THREE.Vector3()` or `new THREE.Color()` inside the `useFrame` / `requestAnimationFrame` loop.
