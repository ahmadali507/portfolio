The page tells one story in about ten sections, from an animated hero to a call to action.

Three kinds of motion carry it: a timed reveal in the hero, entrances that replay as sections scroll back into view, and a five-step walkthrough whose active step follows the scroll.

A sign-up dialog branches by persona, and a separate dialog takes business inquiries.
